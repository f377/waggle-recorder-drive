WAGGLE Recorder Drive

A private utility for archiving recorder files in the operator’s Google Drive and synchronizing verified copies to their desktop.

How it works

The uploader copies completed recorder archives to a designated Drive folder and checks that the copies match. The desktop sync reads those archives, verifies the files, and saves them to the operator’s desktop storage.

Access

The operator authorizes the uploader and desktop sync in Google. The uploader uses Drive read and write permission for archival transfers. The desktop uses Drive read-only permission.

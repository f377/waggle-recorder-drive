WAGGLE Recorder Drive Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Data access and use

WAGGLE Recorder Drive is operated by its owner for a private recorder archive. The app uses Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, file IDs, sizes, and checksums, to locate, copy, and verify archived recorder files. The uploader requests Drive read and write permission. The desktop sync requests Drive read-only permission. Both jobs use the designated recorder archive for their work.

Storage and transfers

The app transfers recorder files between the operator’s server, their designated Google Drive folder, and their desktop. Google handles account sign-in and consent. OAuth credentials are kept in private configuration on the hosts that run the jobs and are used to maintain the operator’s authorized Drive connection. The operator manages access to the archive and its copies.

Retention and control

The operator retains archive copies for their recording and research needs. The uploader checks the Drive archive before removing eligible local recorder payloads. The operator can delete retained copies from Drive and their storage, remove local OAuth credentials, or revoke the app’s Drive permission in their Google Account connections.

Google API Limited Use

WAGGLE Recorder Drive’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Use of the app

The app is for the operator’s authorized archive. Use is subject to the Google APIs Terms of Service. For questions about access, retention, or privacy, use the support contact shown on the app’s Google consent screen.
